What is ALTITUDE

Welcome to Altitude, your ultimate destination for everything snowboarding. Founded by riders, for riders, Altitude was born out of a simple passion: the thrill of carving fresh powder, the freedom of the mountains, and the community that brings it all together. Whether you’re dropping into your very first green run or chasing untracked lines in the backcountry, we’re here to gear you up and keep you inspired. At Altitude, we believe snowboarding is more than just a sport, it's a lifestyle, an escape, and a constant pursuit of the next summit. We curate top-tier gear, share expert riding tips, and deliver the latest snow culture insights so you can make the most of every turn. Step into the cold, find your line, and elevate your ride with Altitude
